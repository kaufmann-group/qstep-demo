Max-Cut Game

Split the nodes. Find a heavier cut.

How large a cut can you find?

Split the nodes into two groups. An edge counts toward your score only when its endpoints have different colors. Aim for the largest total weight of these cut edges—not simply the most edges.

For example, cutting edges of weights 3 and 2 gives a score of 5. The groups do not need to be the same size. You’ll see a success message when you find the maximum cut.
